What to forecast

Overview: Forecast the targets!

The “targets” are time-series of data for use in model development and forecast evaluation. They are updated as new data are made available.

The 1 targets are organised into 1 “themes”. The themes and links to the target files are included below.

Where to start

If you are just getting started, we recommend the following focal sites for each theme. The first site in the list is the recommended starting site.

  • Lakes: fcre, bvre

As you develop your forecasting skills and want to expand to more sites, the targets are available at all 2 sites. You may also consider submitting forecasts to sites that match your interests. For example, a winter class may be more interested in forecasting southern sites than a summer class, which may focus on more northern sites.

More information about the sites can be found in the site metadata.

Explore the targets and themes

In the tables,

  • “duration” is the time-step of the variable, where P1D is a daily mean and P1W is a weekly total.
  • the “horizon” is how far ahead we want you to forecast.
  • the “latency” is the time between data collection and data availability in the target file.
  • Lakes

Daily mean water temperature (°C) at two reservoirs in Virginia: Falling Creek Reservoir (fcre, sensor at 1.6 m) and Beaverdam Reservoir (bvre, sensor at 1.5 m). The observations come from the Virginia Reservoirs LTREB project’s high-frequency sensors, published as the in situ targets of the VERA Forecasting Challenge. VERA measures temperature at many depths; this challenge forecasts one depth per reservoir.

variable duration description horizon latency
temperature P1D Surface Mean Daily Water Temperature (Celsius) 30 days ~ 3 days

The target file is at the following URL. You can load it directly into R:

url <- "https://s3.us-east-005.backblazeb2.com/template-challenge/targets/project_id=templatecast/duration=P1D/lakes-targets.csv.gz"
targets <- readr::read_csv(url, show_col_types = FALSE)
